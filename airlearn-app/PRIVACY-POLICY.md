# Raj Dharma Learn Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 10, 2026  
**App:** Raj Dharma Learn for Android and iOS  
**Website:** https://rajdharma.org/privacy-policy.html

Raj Dharma Learn is a language-learning app with Chinese vocabulary, writing,
video lessons, speech practice, and quizzes. This policy explains what Raj
Dharma ("we", "us", or "our"), a sole proprietor registered in India, collects
and how it is used when you use the app.

## Information we collect

### Account and profile information

When you create an account, we collect your phone number and country dial code
to verify your account using a one-time password (OTP). You may also provide
your name, email address, occupation, and chosen learning language. We do not
ask for your date of birth.

MSG91 processes your phone number to deliver and verify OTPs. Our backend keeps
limited OTP error logs; phone numbers in these logs are masked.

### Learning activity

Favorites, recently viewed words, completed lesson IDs, learned-word counts,
and streak information are stored locally on your device. Your chosen learning
language is stored with your backend profile. The backend also records the
number of free stroke-practice previews used, to enforce the free practice
limit. We do not use these records for advertising.

### Speech practice

Speech Practice requests microphone and speech-recognition permission. When
you use it, your device records speech and uses the operating system's speech
recognition service to produce a transcript. The current app does not upload
the recording or transcript to the Raj Dharma backend for scoring. Speech
recognition and any associated audio handling are provided by your device's
operating system and may be subject to Apple's or Google's terms and privacy
policy. The app currently displays simulated pronunciation scores.

We do not request access to contacts, photos, or location.

### Technical information

When you connect to the service, GoDaddy or other network infrastructure may
process your IP address and request metadata in access or security logs. The
app does not collect a device advertising ID or use those details for
advertising analytics. Infrastructure providers control their own operational
log retention.

### Payments

Payments are processed through Razorpay. We do not receive or store your full
card, bank-account, or UPI credentials. Our backend stores payment order and
transaction references, plan, amount, currency, discount/coupon information,
payment method, status, and timestamps so it can verify purchases and provide
access.

## How we use information

We use information to:

- verify sign-in and maintain your account;
- save your profile and language preference;
- provide learning features and enforce free-preview and paid-access rules;
- process and verify payments, provide purchased access, and address payment
  disputes or legal obligations;
- keep the service secure, troubleshoot delivery failures, and respond to
  privacy or support requests.

Where applicable law requires a lawful basis, processing is based on providing
the account and requested services, consent where required (including
microphone access), compliance with legal obligations, and service-security
interests where recognized by local law.

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral
advertising. We do not currently integrate an advertising, analytics, or
dedicated crash-reporting SDK.

## Service providers and sharing

We share only what is needed to operate the service with:

- **Supabase**, for the database and configured learning-file storage;
- **GoDaddy**, which hosts the backend API;
- **MSG91**, for OTP delivery and verification;
- **Razorpay**, for payment checkout and transaction verification; and
- **Apple or Google speech-recognition services**, when you choose to use
  speech practice.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy
practices. We may also disclose information when required by law or when
necessary to protect users, the service, or our legal rights.

## Retention and account deletion

We keep account and profile information while your account is active. You can
request deletion in **Settings → Account → Delete Account**. Account deletion
removes your profile, entitlements, stroke-preview counter, and locally stored
learning progress. The database migration also removes the legacy deletion
archive containing profile snapshots.

Captured payment order and transaction records are retained only for
applicable tax, accounting, payment-dispute, fraud-prevention, and legal
requirements; uncompleted orders are removed. When an account is deleted, our
backend replaces its account identifier on retained records with random
identifiers so they are no longer linked to the profile.
Razorpay and MSG91 may retain records independently under their own legal and
retention obligations. The backend does not currently apply an automatic
expiry period to captured payment records; they must be reviewed and removed
when no longer required. Information already held by your device's operating
system, infrastructure providers, or included in device backups may need to be
removed through your device or the relevant provider.

## Children and teens

The app is a general-purpose learning app and is not specifically designed or
marketed for children under 13. It may be used by teens and adults. If you are
under the age at which you can consent to data processing in your location,
use the app only with permission from a parent or legal guardian. Where local
law requires verifiable parental consent, that consent must be obtained before
an account is created. We do not ask for or store date of birth and the app
does not currently provide an in-app parental verification flow. A parent or
guardian who believes a child has provided personal information can contact us
to request access or deletion.

## International processing

The backend API and Supabase project are configured in India. Some service
providers, including payment, messaging, and device speech-recognition
providers, may process information in other locations under their own
operations and terms.

## Your choices and rights

You can review or correct profile details in the app's account settings and
request account deletion there. You may also contact us to request access,
correction, deletion, or information about our handling of your data. Depending
on your location, you may have additional rights, including withdrawing
consent, objecting to or restricting processing, requesting portability, or
complaining to a privacy regulator. We will respond as required by applicable
law.

You can deny microphone permission in your device settings; speech-practice
recording and recognition will then be unavailable. You can also remove
locally stored app data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

## Security

We use account authentication, server-side handling of credentials, and
restricted database permissions to protect information. No method of
transmission or storage is completely secure. Production deployments must use
HTTPS and a securely configured signing secret.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or applicable requirements change. We
will update the effective date above and provide additional notice in the app
when required.
